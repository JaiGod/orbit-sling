How to play

  • Drag back anywhere and release to launch your probe, like a slingshot. The dotted line predicts its path.
  • Planets pull with real inverse-square gravity. Fly close for a sharp gravity assist.
  • Pass through every gold ring to unlock the target moon, then reach it.
  • Avoid planet surfaces, asteroids and black-hole horizons. Moons start moving when you launch.
  • On fuel levels, hold the screen mid-flight to fire thrusters toward your finger (or use arrow keys).
  • Fewer launches = more stars. Keys: arrows aim/power, Space launch, R retry, P pause, M mute.
